WellStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stairwell opening stays: well length, well width, trim clear, multi-well counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Calculations stay on device. Purchases use Google Play Billing.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify well length, well width, and trim clear on site.
