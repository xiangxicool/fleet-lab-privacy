Privacy Policy — WireCorner Lab

Last updated: 2026-07-27

WireCorner Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for welded-wire outside-corner reinforcement piece-count estimates.

The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers. Calculations stay on device.

Pro unlock uses Google Play Billing. Purchase records are handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy.

Optional CSV export is user-initiated and shares only what you choose via the system share sheet.

Contact: use the Play Store listing support email for this app.
