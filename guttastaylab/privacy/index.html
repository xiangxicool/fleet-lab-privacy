Privacy Policy — GuttaStay Lab

GuttaStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for gutta drip moulding stays: drop rise, gutter run, spout clear, multi drops, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account. Calculations run on-device. We do not collect personal content from the tools.

If you purchase Pro unlock, Google Play Billing processes the transaction. We do not receive your full payment card details.
