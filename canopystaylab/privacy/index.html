CanopyStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for entry canopy stays: projection depth, fascia height, wall clear, multi-bay counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify projection depth, fascia height, and wall clear on site.
