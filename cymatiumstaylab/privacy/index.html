CymatiumStay Lab — Privacy Policy

CymatiumStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for cymatium crown moulding stays: crown rise, crown run, bed clear, multi-unit counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not show ads, and does not require an account. Purchases use Google Play Billing only.

Field estimate only. Not structural or finish certification.
