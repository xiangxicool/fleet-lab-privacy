KickStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

KickStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair kickboard / plinth stays: kick height, plinth run, toe clear, multi-flight counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
