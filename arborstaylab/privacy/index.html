ArborStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for garden arbor stays: arch span, post height, path clear, multi-arch counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural certification. Verify arch span, post height, and path clear on site.
