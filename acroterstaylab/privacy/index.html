Privacy Policy — AcroterStay Lab

AcroterStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for acroter cornice crest moulding stays: acroter rise, acroter, acroter, multi acroter, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account. Calculations run on-device. We do not collect personal content from the tools.
