Privacy Policy — CabinHook Lab

Last updated: 2026-07-29

CabinHook Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for cabin hook-and-eye fasteners: hook projection, eye ID, screw spacing, multi-leaf counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this inventory build. Calculation inputs and history are stored locally on the device (Android DataStore).

Purchases

Optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Purchase status is restored via Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment card details.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Optional CSV export of your local history is initiated only by you and shared via the Android share sheet you choose.

Children

The App is intended for adult trades users. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use the support email published on the Google Play store listing for privacy questions.
