Privacy Policy

RxCouplerBankStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for rx-coupler-bank stays: bank size, cable run, overlap clear, multi banks, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
