GateStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for yard gate stays: leaf width, hinge rise, latch clear, multi-leaf counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a locksmith or BHMA certification. Verify leaf width, hinge rise, and latch clear on site.
