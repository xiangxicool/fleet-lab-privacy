Privacy Policy — ThresholdSill Lab

Last updated: 2026-07-28

ThresholdSill Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for shower threshold / sill planning: sill height, sill run, face area, ramp-rise face, waste uplift, and pack-count estimates.

Data we do not collect

The App does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs in the shipped build. Calculations and history stay on-device (Android DataStore).

Purchases

Optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app product processed by Google Play Billing. Google may process purchase data under Google’s policies. The App does not receive your payment card details.

Permissions

No dangerous permissions are requested for core calculation features. CSV export uses the system share sheet / FileProvider for files you choose to share.

Contact

Use the support email published on the Play listing once the app is published.
