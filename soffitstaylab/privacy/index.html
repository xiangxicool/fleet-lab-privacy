SoffitStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

SoffitStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair soffit underside stays: soffit run, drop height, joint clear, multi-bay counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
