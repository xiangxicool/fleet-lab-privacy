Privacy Policy

ZigzagBridgeStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for zigzag-bridge stays: bay length, face run, paint clear, multi bays, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
