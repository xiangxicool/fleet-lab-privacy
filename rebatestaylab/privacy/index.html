RebateStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

RebateStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair trim rebate / groove stays: rebate depth, groove width, lip clear, multi-joint counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
