CornerBracket Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

CornerBracket Lab (“the App”) is an offline remodel planner for glass-to-glass / wall-to-glass corner bracket spacing, glass clear, standoff, multi-bracket counts, waste uplift, and pack-count estimates. It is not a structural or tempered-glass certification tool and is distinct from GlassClamp Lab, SideLite Lab, and WallJamb Lab.

Data we collect

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations and history stay on-device. There is no analytics SDK, no ads SDK, and no account system built by us.

Purchases

Optional one-time Pro unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. Purchase status is managed by Google and may restore with your Google account. We never see your payment card details.

Contact

Support contact is listed on the Play Store listing when published. Inventory builds may leave contact TBD.
