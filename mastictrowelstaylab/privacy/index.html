Privacy Policy

MasticTrowelStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for mastic-trowel stays: trowel rise, patch run, skin clear, multi patches, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
