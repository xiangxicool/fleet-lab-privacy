GoingStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair going depth: going depth, nosing projection, undercut clear, multi-flight counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not an ADA, egress, or structural certification. Verify going depth, nosing projection, and undercut clear on site.
