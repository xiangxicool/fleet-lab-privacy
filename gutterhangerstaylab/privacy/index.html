Privacy Policy

GutterHangerStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for gutter-hanger stays: hanger span, eave run, overlap clear, multi hangers, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
