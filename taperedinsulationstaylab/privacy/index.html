Privacy Policy

TaperedInsulationStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for tapered-insulation stays: taper rise, panel run, adhesive clear, multi panels, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
