FrictionStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

FrictionStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for side-hung friction stays: channel length, slider travel, stack height, multi-sash counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the inventory build.

Calculations and history are stored on-device (Android DataStore). Pro unlock is processed by Google Play Billing; purchase state is restored via your Google account.

Field estimate only. Not a BHMA or friction-force certification. Verify channel length, slider travel, and stack height on site.

Contact: use the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.
