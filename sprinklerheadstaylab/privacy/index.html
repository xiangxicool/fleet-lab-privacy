Privacy Policy

SprinklerHeadStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for sprinkler-head stays: orifice size, branch run, overlap clear, multi heads, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
