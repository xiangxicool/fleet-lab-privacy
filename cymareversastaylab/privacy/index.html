CymaReversaStay Lab — Privacy Policy

CymaReversaStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for cyma reversa moulding stays: reversa rise, reversa run, bed clear, multi-unit counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not show ads, and does not require an account. Purchases use Google Play Billing only.

Field estimate only. Not structural or finish certification.
