Privacy Policy — SoftJoint Lab

SoftJoint Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for soft-joint sealant length, volume, and cartridge estimates.

Calculations and history stay on your device. The App does not require an account and does not include advertising SDKs in this build.

Optional Pro unlock uses Google Play Billing. Purchase records are processed by Google under Google’s policies.

Field estimate only — not a waterproofing or structural certificate.

Contact: use the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.
