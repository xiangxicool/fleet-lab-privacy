Privacy Policy

VacuumBreakerStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for vacuum-breaker stays: breaker size, supply run, overlap clear, multi breakers, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
