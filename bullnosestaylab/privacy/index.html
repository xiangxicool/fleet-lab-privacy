BullnoseStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

BullnoseStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair bullnose / rounded tread-end stays: nose radius, tread width, overhang clear, multi-tread counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
