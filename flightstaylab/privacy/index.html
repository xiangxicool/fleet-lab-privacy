FlightStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair flight stays: flight length, rise total, rest clear, multi-flight counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Calculations stay on device. Purchases use Google Play Billing.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify flight length, rise total, and rest clear on site.
