Privacy Policy — HeadroomStay Lab

HeadroomStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair headroom stays: clear height, pitch angle, landing drop, multi-flight counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not require login, and does not show ads. Purchases use Google Play Billing.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify clear height, pitch angle, and landing drop on site.
