PivotStay Lab — Privacy Policy

PivotStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for center-pivot stays: pivot offset, open arc, balance arm, multi-sash counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not show ads, and does not require an account. Purchases use Google Play Billing only.

Field estimate only. Not an egress or BHMA certification.
