LetterPlate Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

LetterPlate Lab (“the App”) is an offline remodel planner for door letter / mail plate slot width, flap height, reveal clear, multi-plate counts, waste uplift, and pack-count estimates. It is not a postal, security, or weatherproof certification tool and is distinct from KickPlate Lab, MortiseFace Lab, DoorPull Lab, and DoorViewer Lab.

Data we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Optional history is stored locally.

Purchases

Pro unlock uses Google Play Billing. Purchase status is managed by Google Play and your Google account. We do not receive your payment card details.

Permissions

Billing only (via Play Billing library). No SMS, contacts, location, microphone, or camera access.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Use the support email published on the Play Store listing.
