Privacy Policy

HypotracheliumStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for column hypotrachelium band stays: band height, shaft girth, seat clear, multi bands, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
