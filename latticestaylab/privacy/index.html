LatticeStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for fence lattice stays: panel width, diamond gap, post clear, multi-panel counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural certification. Verify panel width, diamond gap, and post clear on site.
