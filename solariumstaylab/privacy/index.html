SolariumStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for solarium glazing stays: span width, ridge rise, sill clear, multi-span counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not an egress or BHMA certification. Verify span width, ridge rise, and sill clear on site.
