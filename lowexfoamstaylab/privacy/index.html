Privacy Policy

LowExFoamStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for low-ex-foam stays: nozzle size, pipe run, overlap clear, multi nozzles, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
