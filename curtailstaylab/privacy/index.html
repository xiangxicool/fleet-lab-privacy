CurtailStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for curtail / bullnose step stays: scroll radius, tread width, return clear, multi-step counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. Calculations stay on device. Purchases use Google Play Billing.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify scroll radius, tread width, and return clear on site.
