ApronStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

ApronStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair apron / cover-board stays: apron height, cover run, reveal clear, multi-run counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
