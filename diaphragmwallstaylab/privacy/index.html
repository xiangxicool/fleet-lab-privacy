Privacy Policy

DiaphragmWallStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for diaphragm-wall stays: wall thick, panel run, overlap clear, multi panels, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
