PatioStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for patio paver stays: run length, joint gap, edge clear, multi-run counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or drainage certification. Verify run length, joint gap, and edge clear on site.
