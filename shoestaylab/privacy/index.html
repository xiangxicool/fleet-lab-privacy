ShoeStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair shoe rail stays: shoe length, return height, skirt clear, multi-run counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural or egress certification. Verify shoe length, return height, and skirt clear on site.
