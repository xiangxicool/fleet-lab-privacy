Privacy Policy

VoussoirWedgeStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for voussoir stays: wedge rise, arch run, joint clear, multi voussoirs, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
