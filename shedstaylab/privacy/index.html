ShedStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for garden shed stays: wall length, door clear, corner brace, multi-wall counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural certification. Verify wall length, door clear, and corner brace on site.
