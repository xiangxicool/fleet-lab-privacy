Spindle Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Spindle Lab (“the App”) is an offline remodel planner for door-handle spindle length, square size, door thickness, multi-spindle counts, waste uplift, and pack-count estimates. It is not a security, fire-rating, or locksmith certification tool and is distinct from ThumbTurn Lab, MortiseFace Lab, and Escutcheon Lab.

Data we collect

No account. Calculations stay on device. Optional history stored locally.

Purchases

Pro unlock uses Google Play Billing. We do not receive payment card details.

Permissions

Billing only. No SMS, contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

Children

Not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Use the support email on the Play Store listing.
