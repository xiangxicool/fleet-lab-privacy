Privacy Policy

CavettoMouldStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for cavetto mould stays: cavetto radius, face run, paint clear, multi cavettos, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal data on our servers. Calculations stay on device. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing.
