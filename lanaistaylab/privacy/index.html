LanaiStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for lanai rail stays: rail span, post height, deck clear, multi-bay counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not an egress or BHMA certification. Verify rail span, post height, and deck clear on site.
