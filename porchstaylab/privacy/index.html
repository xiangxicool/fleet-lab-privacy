PorchStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for front porch stays: deck depth, post spacing, step clear, multi-bay counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not an egress or structural certification. Verify deck depth, post spacing, and step clear on site.
