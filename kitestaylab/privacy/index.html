KiteStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

KiteStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for stair kite / winder tread stays: inner going, outer going, wedge clear, multi-kite counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data. Calculations stay on device. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.
