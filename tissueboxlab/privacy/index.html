TissueBox Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

TissueBox Lab (“the App”) is an offline Android calculator for facial tissue box sizing estimates. This policy describes how the App handles information.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. Calculations and history stay on your device. There is no account system and no advertising or analytics SDK in the App.

Purchases

Optional Pro unlock is sold through Google Play Billing (one-time product). Purchase and restore are handled by Google under Google’s policies. We do not receive your payment card details.

Permissions

The App uses only permissions required for Play Billing and normal app operation. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, or SMS.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. It is a general-audience utility.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email listed on the Google Play store listing for TissueBox Lab.
