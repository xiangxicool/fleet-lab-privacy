Privacy Policy — SoffitVent Lab

Last updated: 2026-07-27

SoffitVent Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for soffit vent intake linear-feet estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not include advertising SDKs.

Data we process

  • Calculation inputs and history are stored locally on your device.
  • If you unlock Pro, Google Play Billing processes the purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Optional CSV export shares a file you choose via the Android share sheet.

Data we do not collect

  • No location tracking, no analytics SDK, no ad ID collection by the App.

Contact

Use the support email published on the Google Play store listing.
