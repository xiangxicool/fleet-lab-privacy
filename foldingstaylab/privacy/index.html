FoldingStay Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-30

FoldingStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for folding / multi-segment casement stays: fold stack, arm segment, pivot clearance, multi-sash counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the inventory build.

Calculations and history are stored on-device (Android DataStore). Pro unlock is processed by Google Play Billing; purchase state is restored via your Google account.

Field estimate only. Not a BHMA or security certification. Verify fold stack, arm segment, and pivot clearance on site.

Contact: use the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.
