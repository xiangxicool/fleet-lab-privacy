CarportStay Lab (“the App”) is an offline field calculator for carport bay stays: bay width, beam height, curb clear, multi-bay counts, waste %, and pack estimates.

The App does not collect personal data, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not require a login. Purchase status is handled by Google Play Billing on-device.

Field estimate only. Not a structural certification. Verify bay width, beam height, and curb clear on site.
